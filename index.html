Description

The Alfa Romeo GTV 2000 (Gran Turismo Veloce) stands as one of the finest expressions of Italian sports car design from the early 1970s. Penned by Giorgetto Giugiaro during his time at Bertone, the 105-series body is widely regarded as a high-water mark in postwar European automotive styling — all long bonnet, fastback roofline, and purposeful stance.

This 1972 example, finished in factory Alfa Rosso over cognac leather, presents as a honest, well-maintained driver with an appealing combination of correct specification and sympathetic stewardship. The car spent the bulk of its life in the arid Southwest, where the dry climate has been kind to the floors, sills, and inner structure — the areas that most commonly claim these cars.

The twin-cam 2.0-liter feeds through a pair of Dell'Orto carburetors and pulls cleanly throughout the rev range. The five-speed gearbox shifts precisely with no notchiness or jumping out of gear. Brakes are progressive and confidence-inspiring. This is a car that rewards the driver who simply wants to use it.

Ownership History & Included Items

Originally delivered new in Italy in 1972, the car was imported to the United States in 1976 and has remained in the Southwest ever since. The current seller acquired it in 2018 from a retired engineer in Tucson who had owned the car for fourteen years. The car has been driven and enjoyed, not stored — roughly 3,000 to 4,000 miles per year since 2018.

A partial service history back to 1985 is present, with receipts for work described above. The car's originality has been maintained throughout.

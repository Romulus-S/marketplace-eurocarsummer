Description

Offered Without Reserve

Coming after the iconic 500 and before its more modern iterations, the 126 represents Fiat's last rear-engine and rear wheel drive car. The 126 featured comfort and safety innovations over its predecessor, modernized it with a synchronized four speed transmission (except first gear), and refreshing the interior.

The second series introduced a "Personal 4" trim level that came with new plastic bumpers and matching side trim as well as a wider rear seat. It retained the 594cc engine from the 500 R.

This example, according to its original Italian registration booklet as pictured in the gallery, was first registered on the 24th of May, 1977 to Maria Grazia L'Episcopo in the province of Enna with the license plate EN53187. The seller states that she was a nun who worked at the OASI Institute in Troina, Sicily. Upon her death, ownership was transferred to her daughter, who sold it to the seller.

The odometer shows just 3,024 kilometers, which the seller believes to be original along with its Blu Adriatico paint and black vinyl seats. A service invoice from earlier this year provided in the gallery shows the car received a brake refresh, a new battery, a carburetor rebuild, the gear shift bushing was replaced, and more. The car comes with a spare tire, tool kit, original Fiat factory literature, import paperwork, and a clean Connecticut title.
